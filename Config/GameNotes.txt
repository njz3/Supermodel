; Supermodel game notes: shown on the Game page of the in-game menu (Tab), for
; the game running. Read each time the menu opens.
;
; [name] or [name1, name2, ...] starts the notes of those ROM sets (the names
; of Games.xml). A clone without notes of its own shows its parent's. A game
; named in several sections shows them all, in order.
; Blank lines separate paragraphs. "## Title" is a heading, "- text" a bullet
; point; a line that follows without a blank line continues the paragraph or
; the point. A line starting with ";" is a comment.
;
; "Test mode": press the Test button. In its menus, Service moves the cursor
; and Test selects. Tips marked "not verified" come from a single source or
; from sources that disagree.

[daytona2, dayto2pe]
## Region
The region change menu (Japan, Korea, Australia, Export, USA) opens in two
ways:
- In Test mode, press Service and Test together.
- In Test mode, hold Start and press VR4, VR4, VR2, VR3, VR1, VR3, VR2.

## Race modes
GAME MODE, in GAME ASSIGNMENTS, sets the race length. The Grand Prix and
Endurance modes add tyre wear and pit stops.

## Secrets
- Time Lap (alone on the track, no CPU cars): at the transmission select, hold
  Start and confirm with the accelerator. Linked, every player must do it.
- Other singer: hold Start on the "Gentlemen, start your engines" screen. The
  race plays the singer the operator did not choose (Dennis St. James or
  Takenobu Mitsuyoshi).
- Wheel cameras: VR1 and VR2 together, left front wheel; VR2 and VR3
  together, right front wheel. VR2 again in the VR2 view: cockpit.
- Rocket start (Advanced and Expert, standing starts): hold the brake and keep
  the revs in the yellow zone (about 7000 rpm) during the countdown, then
  release the brake at GO.
- Advanced course, laps 3 and 4: tap Start quickly at the space attraction,
  and the spaceships fire at the track.

[daytona2]
- Reverse courses: at the course select, hold Start and confirm the course
  with the accelerator.
- Hornet, the special car with a tuning screen: put the shifter in 1st before
  inserting a coin, then start. At the course select, shift to 4th and press
  VR1, VR4, VR2, VR4, VR1; shift back to 1st (or press VR2) and choose the
  course. A tuning screen (engine, steering, suspension) replaces the car
  select: the view buttons choose a slider, the wheel moves it. One wrong
  gear or button cancels the sequence.

[dayto2pe]
- Reverse courses (not verified, sources disagree): hold Start while choosing
  the course, or hold the brake at the car select until REVERSE shows.
- The Power Edition adds the Challenge course, the three courses joined into
  one long race.

## Music
The Power Edition plays music remixed by Takenobu Mitsuyoshi. The Dennis
St. James versions of Daytona USA 2 are chosen in Test mode, under GAME
ASSIGNMENTS.

  transmission. Linked, every player must do it.
- Rocket start (Mystery Ruins and Classic Castle, standing starts): hold the
  brake, keep the revs in the lower yellow zone, then release the brake and
  floor it at the start.

[scudplus, scudplusa]

- Super Beginner, the new oval course in a giant house, has joke cars: on
  that course only, hold Start while confirming the car. The Porsche becomes
  a bus full of AM2 staff, the Ferrari a tank, the Viper a cat, the McLaren a
  rocket car. In the race, Start sets off each one's special: horn, cannon,
  meow, or a boost usable three times.

## Region
No region menu: the region is the ROM set's (eca Export, ecau USA, ecaj

## Stretcher mode
To play as the paramedics pushing a stretcher: at the transmission select,
highlight Manual without touching the pedals, then shift Up, Up, Down, Down,
Up. On a cabinet with a 4-way H shifter: 3, 3, 4, 4, 3 or 1, 1, 2, 2, 1
(Supermodel emulates the sequential shifter only). It drives like the
ambulance.

## Other tips
- Start skips the cutscenes.
- "Special" bonus (not verified): case 1, drive through the blue Emergency
  Call Ambulance billboard in the park after the first crossing on the hill
  down to the lake; case 3, take the narrow passage at the far right of the
  petrol station; case 4, after the bridge jump, pass under the burning house
  before the log falls.

[lemans24]
## Region
In Test mode, press Start, Start, Service, Service, Start, Test. In COUNTRY
SELECT MODE, Service changes the country (Japan, USA, Export, Australia) and
Test leaves.

## Cabinet type, drive board and view buttons
The game knows two cabinets, set by CABINET TYPE in Test mode (GAME
ASSIGNMENTS) and kept in the NVRAM. Each has its own drive board (force
feedback) and its own view buttons:
- TWIN-NORMAL: the standard twin cabinet, with the Model 2 drive board of
  Indy 500 (program EPR-18261), and two view buttons, zoom in and zoom out
  (VR1 and VR2 in Supermodel; VR3 and VR4 do nothing).
- TWIN-LEMANS: the "Le Mans" cabinet, with the Model 3 drive board of Scud
  Race (program EPR-19338A), and four view buttons, one per view (VR1 to
  VR4).

The program Supermodel loads is chosen in Config/Games.xml (lemans24's
driveboard_program region: EPR-18261 by default) and must match CABINET TYPE,
otherwise the force feedback is wrong. This front-end's cabinets use
TWIN-NORMAL with EPR-18261.

## Linked cabinets
COMMUNICATION MODE (MASTER, SLAVE, NO LINK) and Cabinet Number, in GAME
ASSIGNMENTS, set each cabinet's place in the link. Entering Test mode on one
linked cabinet puts them all in Test mode.

## Secrets
- The Porsche 917K is the rival when the full 24 hours are completed.
- Secret cars (not verified, sources disagree): at the car select, highlight
  the Nismo GT-R (far left), hold the brake and Start, and press the
  accelerator while holding VR1 and VR3 (Sonic in a buggy) or VR1 and VR2
  (Porsche 917K). Another source: both zoom buttons held while pressing the
  accelerator, then release the brake.

[srally2]
## Region
In Test mode, press Service short and long (held 4 to 5 seconds): 4 short,

## Starting a game
Starting a game before any 3D graphics show (during the Sega logo, for
instance) can cause problems.

## Secrets
- Sega Rally 1 cars (Toyota Celica GT-Four, Lancia Delta HF Integrale): at
  the car select, press View and pull the handbrake together. Only once the
  machine has run more than 30 days and 100 games: not with a new NVRAM.
- Cone game (Riviera, same condition): on lap 1, knock down both red cones
  after the second hairpin. An opening appears on the left: drive through it
  on lap 2 for 30 seconds of knocking cones down, the race timer stopped.
- Music off: after the name entry, hold Start until the race begins.
- Simple display (time difference): after the name entry, hold View and the
  handbrake until the race begins.
- No handicap in a linked race: every player holds the brake from choosing
  the course until the race starts.

with free play. No key held: 3rd person near. P1 Start selects the 1st person
view.

## Starting a game
Wait for the Darth Vader sequence of the attract mode before starting a game:
started before any 3D graphics show, the game crashes before the stage loads.

If the game boots straight to the stage select, Supermodel was quit with
credits in the machine: clear the NVRAM (Alt+N) and reset (Alt+R).
